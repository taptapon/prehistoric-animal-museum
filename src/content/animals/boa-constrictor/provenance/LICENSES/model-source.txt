Use: living boa constrictor (Boa constrictor) exhibit, added under the
owner-approved 2026-11-23 living-species collection expansion. The model is
a photogrammetry scan of a real animal presented as a calm resting coil;
the parent-facing notes record the scan framing and the unverified
Hispaniola population status.

Intake screening (15 prehistoric-snake Sketchfab query groups exhausted,
then modern-snake query groups) is recorded with the full candidate table,

assets/candidates/snake-recon/INTAKE-EVIDENCE.md, following the
realistic-style and prehistoric-first intake standard set by the project

Evolved, Prehistoric Kingdom, Jurassic World Alive), licence-laundered
re-uploads of a NonCommercial original, fossil-specimen scans, untextured
grey meshes, flat relief boards and stylised/cartoon candidates were

oversized (over the 250k-triangle absolute cap) and NonCommercial
candidates were excluded.
