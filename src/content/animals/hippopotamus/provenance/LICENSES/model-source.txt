Use: live reconstruction carrier for the hippopotamus exhibit, the museum's
first living-species exhibit (owner-approved collection expansion,
2026-11-23). The model presents the living common hippopotamus
(Hippopotamus amphibius); no reconstruction simplification is claimed
beyond the standard museum presentation scale.

Direct source verified from the Sketchfab model page and its downloadable
GLB on 2026-11-23. The page displays the Creative Commons Attribution
licence. The same author (sdpm) supplied the reviewed Italian white wolf
used for the dire-wolf exhibit; a 2024 re-upload of this same model by
another account (psk98077, identical vertex count) was identified and the
original 2021 source preferred.

Intake screening (6 Sketchfab queries, 44 unique candidates) found only one
live prehistoric hippopotamus reconstruction (Hippopotamus gorgops by
hippohipo, CC BY) whose 1.65-million-face geometry exceeds the absolute
model budget sixfold; film, game and sculpture derivatives (Madagascar's
Gloria, Planet Zoo, billiard-ball and Egyptian figurine models) were
rejected. Evidence: assets/candidates/hippopotamus-recon/recon/.
